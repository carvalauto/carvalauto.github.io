Signs you need a new center vent grille

If your Pajero's middle air vent is damaged or missing, you'll likely notice one or more of these issues.

  • Visible cracks or breaks in the vent grille plastic
  • The grille is loose or falls out of the dashboard
  • Airflow from the center vent is blocked or uneven due to broken louvers
  • Rattling or buzzing noise from the dashboard area while driving
  • Missing vent grille entirely, leaving an open hole in the dash
  • Faded or peeling wood-color finish on the original grille

A broken vent grille can let debris into the ductwork and may cause rattles; replace it promptly to avoid further dashboard damage.

Installation Guide

Installing this part is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 20-40 minutes.

Tools Required
Plastic trim removal tool, Phillips-head screwdriver (if screws are present), flashlight
1

Remove the old grille

Use a plastic trim removal tool to carefully pry around the edges of the existing center vent grille. Work slowly to avoid scratching the dashboard.

2

Disconnect any connectors

If your old grille has electrical connectors (e.g., for a vent illumination light), unplug them before fully removing the grille.

3

Clean the mounting area

Wipe the vent opening with a clean cloth to remove dust and debris so the new grille seats properly.

4

Test-fit the new grille

Align the new wood-color grille with the vent opening and check that the clips or tabs line up with the dashboard slots.

5

Install the new grille

Press the grille firmly into place until the retaining clips click. If screws are used, secure them with a Phillips-head screwdriver—do not overtighten.

6

Reconnect connectors

If applicable, plug any electrical connectors back into the new grille.

7

Check operation

Turn on the blower and adjust the vent louvers to confirm smooth movement and proper airflow.
