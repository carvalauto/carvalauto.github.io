Signs your cup holder needs replacing

Cup holders fail in a few predictable ways, usually from age, heat, or spilled drinks.

  • The cup holder no longer stays closed or pops open on its own
  • Cracks or broken clips around the mounting edges
  • Drinks wobble or tip because the internal spring or grip is worn
  • The unit rattles over bumps or feels loose in the console
  • Visible discoloration or damage from spilled drinks
  • Missing pieces after a previous removal or repair attempt

If the surrounding console trim is also cracked or loose, fix that at the same time so the new cup holder has a solid mounting surface.

Will the color match my interior exactly?
Red, grey, and black options are listed, but interior plastics fade with age. Compare against your existing trim in daylight; an exact match to a 10-year-old interior is not guaranteed.
