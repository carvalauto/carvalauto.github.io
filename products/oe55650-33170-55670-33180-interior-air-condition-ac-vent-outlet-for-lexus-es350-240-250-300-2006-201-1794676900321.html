What is OEM 55650-33170?

This is a pair of interior air conditioning vent outlets for the Lexus ES350 (2006–2012). They are the dashboard vents that direct heated or air-conditioned air toward the driver and front passenger. The set carries Lexus reference numbers 55650-33170 and 55670-33180, so it is a genuine replacement-style part rather than a universal aftermarket vent.

These vent outlets are designed to replace cracked, broken, or missing dashboard air vents in the 2006–2012 Lexus ES350. Each unit is a complete vent assembly with the directional louvers and housing, so you can swap out a damaged vent without trying to repair individual slats or clips. The parts are new and come as a single item listing, with the two reference numbers covering the left and right positions.

Signs you need a replacement vent outlet

Dashboard vents take a lot of use, and the plastic parts can fail in a few common ways.

  • Airflow from one dashboard vent is weak or blocked
  • Vent louvers are cracked, broken, or missing
  • The vent direction tabs no longer move or stay in place
  • The vent housing is loose or rattles over bumps
  • Visible gaps around the vent where it meets the dashboard
  • The vent cannot be adjusted to direct air toward the driver or passenger

If the vent is loose because of damaged dashboard mounting points rather than the vent itself, a new outlet may not sit securely; inspect the dash opening before ordering.

Installation Guide

Installing this OEM 55650-33170 is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 30–45 minutes.

Tools Required
Trim removal tool, plastic pry bar, Phillips-head screwdriver, microfiber cloth
1

Prepare the workspace

Park the car, turn off the ignition, and lay a microfiber cloth over the dashboard and center console to protect the trim.

2

Release the old vent

Use a plastic trim removal tool to gently pry around the edge of the vent housing, working slowly to release the retaining clips without scratching the dash.

3

Disconnect and remove

Pull the vent straight out of the dashboard opening. If the vent has an attached air duct or linkage, release it carefully and set the old unit aside.

4

Clean the opening

Wipe out any dust or debris from the dashboard opening and check that the mounting clips and screw points are intact.

5

Test-fit the new outlet

Align the new vent with the opening and check that the louvers and direction tabs move freely before pushing it into place.

6

Seat the new vent

Press the vent firmly into the dashboard until the retaining clips click into place. Do not force it; if it resists, recheck the alignment.

7

Check operation

Turn on the blower and adjust the vent through its full range to confirm airflow and that the louvers stay where you set them.
