What is this Dual USB Type C Charger PD QC3.0 for Toyota Corolla Levin an?

This is an aftermarket dual-port USB charger designed to replace the factory blank or switch panel in select Toyota vehicles. It provides both USB Type-C Power Delivery (PD) and Quick Charge 3.0 (QC3.0) outputs, letting you fast-charge modern phones and tablets from your car's 12V electrical system.

Built to fit into the switch blank on the dashboard of a Toyota Corolla, Levin, or Sienna, this charger module adds two USB ports without altering the interior layout. The dual-button design gives you one Type-C PD port and one QC3.0 port, so you can charge two devices at once—often at full speed, depending on the device and cable. The module is intended for 12V systems and is a straightforward upgrade for cars that came without USB charging from the factory. The housing is sized to match the standard switch opening, and the push-button style means it clicks into place like an original switch. It's a practical add-on for daily drivers, rideshare vehicles, or anyone tired of slow-charging through a cigarette-lighter adapter. The part is new and comes with a one-year warranty. Note that this is an aftermarket accessory, not an replacement, and no factory-style part number is associated with it.

Compatibility Check: Fitment is confirmed for Toyota Corolla 2014–2016; verify that your Levin or Sienna has a compatible switch blank before ordering, as trim levels and model years can vary.

Signs You Need This Charger

If your Toyota lacks a working USB port or you're stuck with slow charging, this module solves those issues.

  • No USB charging port in the vehicle from the factory
  • Existing 12V socket charger falls out or charges very slowly
  • You need to charge two devices at once but only have one port
  • Factory blank switch panel is unused and you want a functional upgrade
  • Devices show 'slow charging' or don't charge while using GPS
  • Cables overheat or charging stops intermittently with a cheap adapter

If your vehicle's 12V outlet has no power at all, check the fuse before replacing the charger module.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will this charger fit my 2015 Corolla LE?
It is listed for Toyota Corolla 2014–2016. However, fitment can depend on whether your trim has the standard switch blank. Compare the opening shape and size to the product's 8x4x2 cm package dimensions before ordering.
Does it support fast charging for iPhone?
The USB-C PD port can fast-charge compatible iPhones with a USB-C to Lightning cable. The QC3.0 port is mainly for Android devices that support Quick Charge.
Is this an factory-style Toyota part?
No. This is an aftermarket upgrade part. It does not carry an factory-style part number and is not manufactured by Toyota.
Do I need to cut or modify the dashboard?
No cutting is required if your vehicle has a standard switch blank. The module is designed to fit into the existing opening. If there's no blank, you may need a different mounting solution.
What is the warranty?
The product includes a one-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
Can I use both ports at the same time?
Yes, both ports can be used simultaneously. Charging speed may be reduced if both devices draw high current, as the total output is shared.
