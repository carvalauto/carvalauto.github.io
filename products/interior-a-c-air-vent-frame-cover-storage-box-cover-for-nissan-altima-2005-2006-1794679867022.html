Signs You Need a New Vent Frame Cover

If you notice any of these issues with your Altima's center dashboard trim, it may be time for a replacement.

  • Visible cracks or breaks in the plastic vent frame
  • Storage box cover missing or not latching properly
  • Faded or discolored trim that doesn't match the rest of the interior
  • Loose or rattling vent frame
  • Pieces of plastic missing or broken off

A damaged vent frame can lead to further interior issues, so address it promptly.

Prepare the area

Turn off the ignition and open the center console. Use a trim removal tool to gently pry off the existing vent frame cover, starting from the edges.

2

Remove old cover

Once the clips are released, carefully pull the old cover away and disconnect any electrical connectors if present (e.g., for vent controls).

3

Wipe the mounting area with a clean cloth to remove dust and debris.

4

Test fit new cover

Position the new cover in place without fully clipping it to ensure proper alignment.

5

Install new cover

Press the new cover firmly into place, engaging all clips. Reconnect any electrical connectors.

6

Verify operation

Check that the vent frame is secure and the storage box cover opens and closes smoothly.
