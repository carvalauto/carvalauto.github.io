Signs you need a new dash vent

A damaged vent can affect both airflow and the look of your dashboard.

  • Louvers are broken, missing, or won’t stay in the position you set.
  • The vent rattles or whistles while the blower is running.
  • Airflow is stuck on one setting or cannot be directed.
  • The outer trim ring is cracked or loose in the dash.
  • The vent has fallen out of the dashboard opening.
  • You notice a gap around the vent where air escapes.

If the vent is cracked or loose, replace it soon to prevent air leaks and further damage to the dashboard opening.

Remove the old vent

Use a plastic trim tool to gently pry around the edges of the old vent until it pops out. If screws are visible, remove them first with a Phillips-head screwdriver.

2

Clean the opening

Wipe the dash opening with a clean cloth to remove dust and debris so the new vent seats properly.

3

Test-fit the new vent

Align the new vent with the opening and check that the mounting tabs line up with the dash slots.

4

Install the new vent

Press the vent firmly into place until the tabs click. If screws were used, reinstall them snugly—do not overtighten.

5

Check operation

Move the louvers through their full range and turn on the blower to confirm airflow and that the vent is secure.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will this vent fit both the driver and passenger sides?
The listing is for a single vent. Fitment depends on the original part number and the specific opening; check that your old vent matches 55604-12160 or 5506112230. Order the quantity you need for each position.
Do I need any special tools to install it?
A plastic trim removal tool is helpful to avoid scratching the dash. If your original vent is held by screws, a Phillips-head screwdriver is also needed.
Is this an original Toyota or BYD part?
This is an aftermarket replacement part with reference numbers 55604-12160 and 5506112230. It is not Genuine Toyota or BYD, but it is designed to fit the listed models.
Can I paint the vent to match my interior?
The vent is made of black ABS plastic. If you choose to paint it, use a plastic-friendly paint and follow the paint manufacturer’s instructions. Proper surface preparation is important for adhesion.
What if the vent feels loose after installation?
Check that all mounting tabs are engaged and that no screws are missing. If the opening is damaged, the vent may not seat correctly; in that case, inspect the dash for cracks or broken clips.
