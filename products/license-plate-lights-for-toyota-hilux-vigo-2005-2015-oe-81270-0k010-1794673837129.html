Signs your plate lamp needs replacing

These are the common failures on the Hilux VIGO plate light assembly.

  • The plate is dark or dim at night even with a new bulb
  • The lamp flickers when you close the tailgate or go over bumps
  • The lens is cracked or cloudy, letting water into the housing
  • The bulb holder is corroded or the contacts are green
  • The housing is loose or a mounting tab is broken
  • The lamp fails to come on with the parking lights

A plate light that stays out is a legal defect in most regions and can fail an inspection, so fix it promptly.

Installation Guide

Installing this OEM 81270-0K010 is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 15–25 minutes.

Tools Required
Trim removal tool, Phillips screwdriver, 10mm socket or nut driver, clean cloth
1

Disconnect the battery

Switch off the ignition and disconnect the negative battery terminal so you are not working on a live circuit.

2

Access the lamp

Open the tailgate and locate the plate lamp housing in the rear bumper or tailgate handle area. On some VIGO models you reach it from behind the bumper; on others the housing unclips from the outside.

3

Release the housing

Use a trim removal tool to gently pry the housing out, or remove the screws or 10mm nuts holding it, depending on the fixing style on your truck. Keep the old screws if they are in good condition.

4

Unplug the connector

Press the release tab on the wiring connector and pull it free from the lamp housing. Do not pull by the wires.

5

Transfer or fit the bulb

Remove the bulb from the old housing and fit it to the new one, or install a new bulb of the correct type. Wipe the bulb with a clean cloth so you do not leave fingerprints on the glass.

6

Fit the new lamp

Plug the connector into the new housing, then seat the housing into the bumper or tailgate and refit the screws or nuts. Do not overtighten plastic fixings.

7

Test the light

Reconnect the battery and switch on the parking lights. Check that the plate is evenly lit and that the lamp does not flicker when you tap the bumper lightly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does this fit my 2012 Hilux VIGO?
It is listed for the 2005–2015 VIGO range with reference 81270-0K010. The safest check is to look at the number stamped on your current lamp housing or compare the shape with the photo before ordering.
Is a bulb included?
The listing is for the lamp assembly as a single item. Unless your supplier states otherwise, reuse the bulb from your old lamp or buy the correct bulb separately.
Can I replace just the lens instead of the whole unit?
On this type of assembly the lens is bonded to the housing, so a cracked lens usually means replacing the complete lamp. That also renews the bulb holder and contacts.
Why does my new lamp still flicker?
Check the bulb first, then inspect the wiring connector for corrosion or a loose terminal. A flicker that continues after fitting a new housing often points to the harness side rather than the lamp.
Do I need to disconnect the battery?
It is not strictly required for a bulb-level job, but it is good practice on any lighting circuit and only takes a moment. It also protects you if the connector is stiff or damaged.
