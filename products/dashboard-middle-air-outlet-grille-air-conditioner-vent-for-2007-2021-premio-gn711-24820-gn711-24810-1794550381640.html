Signs your center vent grille needs replacement

A failing center vent is usually a mechanical or cosmetic problem you can see and feel at the dashboard.

  • Cracked or broken plastic around the vent opening
  • One or more louver vanes are missing or snapped
  • The vent no longer stays aimed where you set it
  • Rattling or buzzing from the center dash while driving
  • The grille sits loose or pops out of the dashboard
  • Visible gaps around the vent that let air escape

If the vent is loose, avoid forcing it back in, because the retaining tabs or dashboard opening can crack.

Installation Guide

Installing this OEM 55670-20290 is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 20-40 minutes.

Tools Required
Trim removal tool, plastic pry bar, Phillips screwdriver (if dashboard trim screws are present), clean microfiber cloth
1

Prepare the dashboard

Switch off the vehicle and let the dashboard area cool. Wipe the center vent surround clean so you can see the seam between the grille and the dash trim.

2

Release surrounding trim

Use a plastic trim tool to gently pry the trim panel around the center vent. Work slowly around the edges and keep the clips with the panel.

3

Remove the old grille

Once the trim is loose, press the vent retaining tabs or release the screws, depending on what your dashboard uses, and pull the old grille straight out.

4

Compare the new part

Set the old and new grilles side by side. Check the reference number, the overall shape, and the louver layout before installing.

5

Seat the new vent

Line up the new grille with the opening and press it in evenly until the retaining tabs click into place. Do not force it at an angle.

6

Refit the trim

Reinstall the surrounding dashboard trim and press the clips back into place. Check that the vent sits flush and the louvers move freely.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this a genuine OEM part?
The listing carries reference number 55670-20290, which is the number to compare against your original vent. Treat it as a replacement part and confirm the number and shape match before ordering.
Will this fit a Toyota Prado?
The title and fitment data list this vent for 2007–2021 Premio models. Some supplier attributes mention Prado, but those come from a generic template and should not be used for fitment. Match your dashboard opening and the reference number instead.
Do I need to remove the whole dashboard?
No. In most cases the center vent can be released by removing the trim around it and pressing the vent retaining tabs. Removing the entire dashboard is not part of a normal vent replacement.
Does the vent come with the louvers?
The product is listed as a complete air outlet grille in gray. The adjustable louvers are part of the vent assembly shown for this location.
Can I install this myself?
Yes, this is a beginner-level trim job if you use plastic trim tools and work slowly. The main risk is cracking old dashboard clips, so avoid metal tools on the trim.
Why does my new vent rattle?
A rattle usually means a retaining tab is not fully seated or a surrounding trim clip is loose. Recheck that the grille is pressed in evenly and that the trim panel is fully clipped.
