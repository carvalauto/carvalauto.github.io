Signs you need a new switch panel

A damaged switch panel can cause the window switches to sit loose or stop working reliably.

  • Cracks or breaks in the plastic around the window switches
  • Switches feel loose or push through the panel
  • Panel no longer clips securely into the door trim
  • Faded or discolored gray finish
  • Missing pieces or broken mounting tabs
  • Windows intermittently stop working due to poor switch seating

If the window switches themselves are faulty, replace the switches as well; this listing is for the panel only.

Installation Guide

Installing this OEM 74232-60050-E0 is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 20-30 minutes.

Tools Required
Trim removal tool, Phillips screwdriver, clean cloth
1

Disconnect battery

Turn off the ignition and disconnect the negative battery terminal to avoid shorting the window circuit.

2

Remove door trim

Pry off the door armrest or switch surround using a trim removal tool, taking care not to scratch the door panel.

3

Unplug switches

Press the release tab on the window switch harness connector and pull it free from the old panel.

4

Remove old panel

Release the retaining clips or remove the screws holding the old panel, then lift it out.

5

Transfer switches

If reusing the original switches, snap them into the new panel in the same orientation.

6

Install new panel

Seat the new panel into the door trim, pressing evenly until all clips engage, and refit any screws.

7

Reconnect and test

Plug the harness back in, reconnect the battery, and test each window switch for smooth operation.
