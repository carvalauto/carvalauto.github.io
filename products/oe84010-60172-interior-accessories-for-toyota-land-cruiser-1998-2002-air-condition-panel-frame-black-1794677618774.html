Signs You Need a New A/C Panel Frame

A worn or broken panel frame can cause fitment and appearance issues around your Land Cruiser’s climate controls.

  • Visible cracks or breaks in the plastic bezel
  • The A/C control panel sits loose or rattles
  • Clips or mounting tabs are snapped off
  • Faded or scratched black finish
  • Gaps around the climate control unit
  • The panel frame no longer holds the controls securely

If the frame is cracked or loose, the A/C panel may not stay in place; replace it before it causes damage to the control unit.
