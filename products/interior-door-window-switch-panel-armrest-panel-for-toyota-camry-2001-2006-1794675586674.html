Signs You Need a New Switch Panel

These symptoms indicate the door window switch panel or armrest trim may need replacement.

  • Cracks or breaks in the plastic around the window switches
  • Loose or sunken window switches that no longer sit flush
  • Faded, peeling, or discolored woodgrain or grey finish
  • Rattles or squeaks from the door panel area
  • Difficulty pressing the window switches due to a damaged bezel
  • Visible gaps between the panel and the door trim

If you notice any of these signs, replace the panel promptly to avoid further damage to the switch assembly or door trim.
