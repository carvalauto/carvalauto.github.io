Signs your vent grille needs replacing

A damaged interior vent grille is usually easy to spot once you know what to look for.

  • Visible cracks or missing pieces in the vent grille
  • Vent louvers that will not stay in the position you set
  • Broken or missing adjustment tabs on the vent
  • Rattling or buzzing from the dashboard vent area
  • A gap around the vent where the grille no longer sits flush
  • Airflow that cannot be aimed because the grille is loose

If the vent housing itself is cracked or the duct behind it is damaged, a grille alone may not fix the problem; inspect the surrounding dashboard area before ordering.

Installation Guide

Installing this OEM 95463834 is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 15–30 minutes.

⏱️
Estimated Time
15–30 minutes
🔧
Skill Level

🛠️
Tools Required
Plastic trim pry tool, Phillips-head screwdriver (if trim panel screws are present), flashlight
1

Inspect the old grille

With the engine off, look at the damaged vent and note how the grille is seated in the dashboard. Check for any screws or trim panels that must come out first.

2

Remove surrounding trim if needed

On some dashboard layouts, a trim bezel around the vent must be unclipped or unscrewed. Use a plastic pry tool to release clips gently, starting at one corner.

3

Release the old grille

Use the pry tool to work around the edge of the old grille and release its retaining clips. Pull it straight out, taking care not to scratch the dashboard.

4

Clean the vent opening

Wipe out dust and debris from the vent opening and check that the duct behind it is intact and not cracked.

5

Test-fit the new grille

Line up the new grille with the opening and check that the clips and alignment tabs match the old part before pushing it in.

6

Seat the new grille

Press the grille into place evenly around its edge until the clips click. Do not force it; if it resists, check alignment and try again.

7

Reinstall trim and check operation

Refit any trim bezel or screws you removed, then move the vent louvers through their range to confirm they adjust smoothly and the grille sits flush.
