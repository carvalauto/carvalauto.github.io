Signs You Need a New Armrest Cover

These are the common issues that lead Hilux owners to replace or cover their door armrests.

  • Visible cracks or splits in the original armrest material
  • Faded or discolored surface from sun exposure
  • Peeling or flaking leather or vinyl
  • Worn-through spots where elbows rest most often
  • Sticky or deteriorating surface texture
  • Tears or holes that catch on clothing

If the underlying armrest structure is broken or the door panel is damaged, a cover alone may not be enough—inspect the base before ordering.
