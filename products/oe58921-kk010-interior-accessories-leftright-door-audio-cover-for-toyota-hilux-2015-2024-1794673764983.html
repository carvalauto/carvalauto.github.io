When to replace a door audio cover

These are the common signs that a Hilux door speaker cover needs attention.

  • Visible crack or split in the door speaker cover
  • Cover is loose and rattles or buzzes while driving
  • Scratches or discoloration that cannot be cleaned off
  • Missing cover leaving the speaker opening exposed
  • Cover clips are broken so it no longer sits flush
  • Door panel looks unfinished after interior or audio work

A loose or missing cover lets dust and moisture reach the speaker area, so address it before the door internals are affected.

Installation Guide

Installing this OEM 58921-KK010 is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 20-40 minutes.

Tools Required
Plastic trim removal tool, Phillips screwdriver, clean microfiber cloth
1

Prepare the door panel

Open the door and inspect the existing cover. If a screw or fastener holds the door card near the speaker, remove it with the Phillips screwdriver.

2

Release the old cover

Work the plastic trim tool under the edge of the old cover and gently pry around the perimeter until the clips release. Do not lever against the speaker cone.

3

Clean the mounting area

Wipe the door panel and speaker opening with a microfiber cloth to remove dust and loose debris before fitting the new cover.

4

Test-fit the new cover

Align the new left or right cover with the speaker opening and check that the clip positions line up with the door card.

5

Seat the clips

Press the cover into place around the edge, working evenly until the clips engage and the cover sits flush with the door panel.

6

Repeat on the other door

Follow the same steps for the opposite side, then check both covers for movement and confirm the door card is secure.
