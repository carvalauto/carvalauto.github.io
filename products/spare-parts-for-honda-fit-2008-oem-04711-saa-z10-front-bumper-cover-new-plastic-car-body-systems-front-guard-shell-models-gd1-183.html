Signs Your Vehicle Bumper Needs Replacement

The following symptoms may indicate the need for replacement:

  • Visible cracks or impact damage on the bumper — compromises structural integrity and crash protection for your your vehicle
  • Bumper sagging or misaligned with body panels — broken mounting tabs causing poor fit
  • Paint peeling, fading, or blistering — UV damage or poor previous repair
  • Mounting clips or tabs broken off — bumper rattles at speed or does not stay securely attached to your your vehicle
  • Rust or corrosion on metal bumper components — structural weakening that can fail in a subsequent impact

Continuing to operate with these symptoms risks further damage. Replace promptly.
