What is this OEM 2011-2016 Toyota Innova DVD Head Unit Dashboard Trim Pan?

This is an aftermarket dashboard trim cover and head-unit mounting frame designed for 2011–2016 Toyota Innova models. It replaces the factory radio surround and center stack trim so you can install a double-DIN DVD or video head unit while keeping a finished, integrated look.

This grey interior dashboard panel trim covers the DVD video head unit opening for 2011-2016 Toyota Innova and Fortuner models. It is an OEM standard size panel with left-hand drive installation and IATF16949 certification. The part is supplied with a 1-year warranty.

Installation Guide

Installing this part is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 45–90 minutes.

⏱️
Estimated Time
45–90 minutes
🔧
Skill Level
Intermediate
🛠️
Tools Required
Plastic trim removal pry tools, Phillips screwdriver, socket set, panel clip remover, clean microfiber cloth
1

Disconnect the battery

Turn off the ignition and disconnect the negative battery terminal before working on the dashboard electrical area.

2

Remove the factory trim

Use plastic pry tools to carefully release the clips holding the original radio surround and center trim. Work slowly to avoid cracking the dash.

3

Remove the old head unit

Take out the screws or brackets securing the original radio, unplug the connectors, and set the old unit aside.

4

Test-fit the new frame

Hold the aftermarket trim frame in place without forcing it. Check that the clip locations and screw holes line up with the dashboard opening.

5

Mount the new head unit

Secure the DVD/video head unit to the frame or dashboard brackets using the supplied or appropriate screws. Reconnect the wiring harness and antenna.

6

Install the trim panel

Press the grey trim cover into place, engaging the clips evenly. Do not overtighten screws; snug is enough to hold the panel without distorting it.

7

Reconnect and test

Reconnect the negative battery terminal, turn on the ignition, and test the head unit. Check that the trim sits flush and all clips are engaged.
