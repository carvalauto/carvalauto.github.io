Signs you need a new shift gearbox trim panel

The trim panel around the shifter can wear out or break over time, and these are the common signs that it needs replacement.

  • Visible cracks or breaks in the plastic around the shift lever
  • The panel is loose or rattles while driving
  • Clips are broken and the panel does not sit flush
  • Faded or peeling wooden finish
  • Gaps between the panel and the center console
  • The panel interferes with shifting or catches on the lever

If the panel is loose, it can trap debris or interfere with the shift lever; replace it promptly to avoid damage to the shifter mechanism.

Installation Guide

Installing this OEM 58804-20370 is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 30-45 minutes.

Tools Required
Plastic trim removal tool, Phillips screwdriver, clean cloth
1

Prepare the vehicle

Park on a level surface, set the parking brake, and turn off the ignition. Move the shift lever to the rearward position to create working space.

2

Remove the old panel

Use a plastic trim removal tool to carefully pry up the edges of the old trim panel, starting at the rear and working forward. Disconnect any electrical connectors if present.

3

Clean the mounting area

Wipe the console surface with a clean cloth to remove dust and debris. Check that all mounting clips are intact and replace any that are broken.

4

Test-fit the new panel

Position the new panel over the shifter and align the clip locations. Do not force it; the panel should sit flush with gentle pressure.

5

Secure the panel

Press down firmly around the edges until the clips engage. Reinstall any screws that were removed.

6

Check operation

Move the shift lever through all gears to ensure the panel does not interfere. Verify that the panel is secure and sits evenly.
