Signs you need a replacement sun visor

A worn or failing sun visor can be annoying and even unsafe when it will not stay in place.

  • Visor droops or falls down on its own
  • Hinge is loose and cannot hold the visor up
  • Fabric is ripped, frayed, or heavily stained
  • Visor is missing or was removed and not replaced
  • Mounting bracket is cracked or broken
  • Color no longer matches the interior trim

Do not drive with a visor that blocks your forward view; replace a drooping or broken visor promptly.

Installation Guide

Installing this part is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 15–30 minutes.

⏱️
Estimated Time
15–30 minutes
🔧
Skill Level
Beginner
🛠️
Tools Required
Phillips screwdriver, trim removal tool (optional), clean cloth
1

Remove the old visor

Lower the visor and remove the screws holding the mounting bracket to the headliner. Support the visor as you remove the last screw so it does not fall.

2

Disconnect the pivot

If your original visor has a separate pivot arm on the opposite side, gently pry off the retaining clip or unscrew the pivot mount.

3

Compare mounting points

Hold the new visor next to the old one and check that the screw holes and pivot arm match. Do not force anything if the layout differs.

4

Attach the new visor

Position the new visor on the headliner and start the screws by hand. Tighten them evenly until the bracket is snug, but do not overtighten.

5

Secure the pivot

Clip or screw the opposite pivot arm into place, making sure the visor swings up and down smoothly.

6

Test the movement

Fold the visor up and down several times to confirm it stays in position and does not interfere with the headliner or mirror.
