Signs You Need New Door Armrests

These symptoms indicate that your Hilux Revo’s interior door armrests are worn or damaged and may need replacement.

  • Visible cracks or splits in the armrest padding or plastic base
  • Loose armrest that moves when you rest your arm on it
  • Broken or missing mounting clips or screw tabs
  • Faded, discolored, or peeling surface material
  • Exposed door panel underneath where the armrest has broken away
  • Rattling or squeaking from the door panel while driving

If an armrest is loose or cracked, avoid leaning on it until it is replaced, as it may break completely and leave sharp edges exposed.

Installation Guide

Installing this OEM 74210-0K060 is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 30-45 minutes per door.

Tools Required
Phillips-head screwdriver, trim removal tool, socket set (if screws are recessed), clean cloth
1

Remove the old armrest

Open the door and locate the screws holding the armrest. Remove any screw covers or caps, then unscrew the fasteners. Gently lift the armrest away from the door panel; if it resists, use a trim removal tool to release the clips.

2

Clean the mounting area

Wipe the door panel surface where the armrest mounts with a clean cloth to remove dirt and loose debris.

3

Test-fit the new armrest

Set the new armrest in place without tightening screws to confirm the mounting holes align with the door panel.

4

Secure the armrest

Start all screws by hand to avoid cross-threading, then tighten them evenly. Do not overtighten, as the plastic base can crack.

5

Reinstall covers

Replace any screw covers or caps that were removed in step 1.

6

Check operation

Press firmly on the armrest to confirm it is secure and does not move. Repeat the process for each door as needed.
