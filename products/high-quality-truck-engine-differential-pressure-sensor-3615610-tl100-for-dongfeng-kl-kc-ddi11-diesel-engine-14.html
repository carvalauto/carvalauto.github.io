Signs Your Vehicle Engine Needs Replacement

The following symptoms may indicate the need for replacement:

  • Excessive black or blue smoke from exhaust — indicates worn piston rings or failed valve stem seals in your your vehicle
  • Knocking or tapping noise from the engine block — typically caused by worn connecting rod bearings in your your vehicle
  • Significant power loss under load — compression loss from worn cylinder liners - common in high-mileage your vehicle engines
  • Coolant mixing with engine oil (milky oil) — cracked block or failed head gasket allowing coolant into oil galleries
  • Engine will not start or stalls repeatedly — after ruling out fuel pump and ECU issues, internal mechanical failure is the likely cause
  • Visible cracks or warping on the engine block — from severe overheating or fatigue; not repairable in the field

Continuing to operate with these symptoms risks further damage. Replace promptly.
