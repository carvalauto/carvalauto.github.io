Front Windshield Fender Seal Cover OEM 53866-0K050 for Toyota Hilux Revo 2015-2024

What is OEM 53866-0K050?

This is the front windshield fender seal cover (also called a wiper seal cover) for the Toyota Hilux Revo, part number 53866-0K050. It is the plastic trim piece that sits at the base of the windshield on the fender side, covering the wiper linkage area and sealing the gap between the hood, fender, and glass.

Front windshield fender seal cover with wiper seal, OEM 53866-0K050, made for replacement and repair on Toyota Hilux Revo models from 2015 to 2024. The part fits at the windshield-to-fender joint and seals the wiper area against water and debris. Supplied as a single item from China, it matches the OEM number for direct fitment.

Signs you need a new seal cover

Look for these common issues that indicate the original cover is failing.

  • Visible cracks or breaks in the plastic cover
  • Water leaking into the engine bay near the wiper area
  • Loose or missing cover that no longer clips in place
  • Rattling or buzzing noise from the cowl area while driving
  • Leaves and debris collecting under the hood near the wipers
  • Faded or discolored plastic that looks worn

A damaged seal cover can let water reach the wiper motor and electrical components, so replace it promptly.

Remove the wiper arm

Use a socket to remove the nut holding the wiper arm on the affected side, then lift the arm off the linkage.

3

Release the old cover

Use a trim removal tool to carefully pry up the clips or remove any screws holding the old seal cover in place.

4

Wipe away dirt and debris from the cowl and fender channel where the new cover will sit.

Align the new seal cover with the mounting points and press it into place, ensuring all clips engage.

6

Reinstall the wiper arm

Slide the wiper arm back onto the linkage, tighten the nut, and check that the wiper operates correctly.
