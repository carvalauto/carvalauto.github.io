What is OEM 63670-60070?

Signs your Prado LC120 roof lamp needs replacement

A failing ceiling light usually shows up as one of these cabin-lighting problems.

  • Dome light does not come on when a door is opened
  • Light flickers or cuts out over bumps
  • Lens is cracked, yellowed, or missing
  • Switch feels loose or will not stay in position
  • Bulb contacts are corroded or the housing is melted
  • Light stays on constantly and drains the battery

Before replacing the lamp, check the bulb and the door-switch circuit first, since a good lamp can appear dead if the fuse or switch is at fault.

Is this a genuine Toyota part?

Will this fit my 2005 Prado LC120?
The listing covers the Prado LC120 from 2003 to 2009, which includes 2005. The supplied fitment data is generic, so confirm your vehicle is an LC120 and compare the old lamp to the reference number before ordering.
Does it come with the bulb included?
The listing describes a complete roof lamp assembly, but it does not state whether a bulb is included. Check the bulb in your existing unit and reuse or replace it as needed.
Can I replace just the lens instead of the whole lamp?
If only the lens is cracked and the housing and switch are fine, a lens alone may be enough. This part is the full assembly, which is the better choice when the switch or housing is also worn or damaged.
Why does my new lamp not light up?
Check the fuse for the interior lighting circuit and the door switches before assuming the lamp is faulty. Also confirm the connector is fully seated and the bulb is good.
