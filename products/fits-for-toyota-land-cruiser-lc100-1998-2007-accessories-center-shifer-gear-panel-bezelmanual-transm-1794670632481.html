Signs You Need a New Shift Panel Bezel

A damaged or missing bezel is usually obvious once you look closely at the center console.

  • Visible cracks or breaks in the plastic trim around the shifter
  • Faded or discolored surface that no longer matches the console
  • Loose bezel that rattles or lifts away from the console
  • Missing pieces of trim around the shift boot opening
  • Gaps that expose the console interior or wiring
  • Difficulty keeping the shift boot properly seated

If the bezel is loose, check that the console clips and mounting points are intact before installing a replacement, as broken clips can cause the new bezel to sit poorly.

1

Park and prepare

Park on a level surface, set the parking brake, and shift the transmission to a position that gives you room to work around the shifter.

2

Remove the old bezel

Use a trim removal tool to gently pry up the edges of the existing bezel, working slowly around the perimeter to release the retaining clips.

3

Disconnect any attachments

If the old bezel has clips or tabs attached to the shift boot, release them carefully and set the boot aside.

4

Clean the mounting area

Wipe the console surface clean with a cloth to remove dust and debris so the new bezel seats evenly.

5

Test-fit the new bezel

Set the new bezel in place without forcing it, checking that the shifter opening and boot align with the console.

6

Secure the bezel

Press the bezel down evenly around the edges until the clips engage, and refit the shift boot if it was removed.

The listing is for the bezel as a single item. Any original clips or fasteners from your existing console should be reused unless otherwise stated.

Yes. Installation is beginner-level and typically takes 20 to 40 minutes with basic trim tools. No special equipment is required.
