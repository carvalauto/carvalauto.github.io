Признаки того, что ваш Honda Lighting нужна замена

The following symptoms may indicate the need for replacement:

  • Фара or tail light not turning on — burned-out bulb, failed LED driver, or corroded socket on your Honda
  • Flickering or intermittent operation — poor ground connection, failing relay, or damaged wiring
  • Condensation inside the housing — failed seal allowing moisture ingress
  • Yellowed or clouded lens — UV degradation reduces light output by up to 70%
  • Cracked or broken housing — allows water entry, risks short circuit
