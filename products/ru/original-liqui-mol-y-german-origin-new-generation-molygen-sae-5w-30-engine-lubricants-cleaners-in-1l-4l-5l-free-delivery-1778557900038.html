Признаки того, что ваш Universal Двигатель нужна замена

The following symptoms may indicate the need for replacement:

  • Excessive black or blue smoke from exhaust — indicates worn piston rings or failed valve stem seals in your Universal
  • Knocking or tapping noise from the двигатель block — typically caused by worn connecting rod bearings in your Universal
  • Significant power loss under load — compression loss from worn cylinder liners - common in high-mileage Universal двигательs
  • Coolant mixing with двигатель oil (milky oil) — cracked block or failed head gasket allowing coolant into oil galleries
  • Двигатель will not start or stalls repeatedly — after ruling out fuel pump and ECU issues, internal mechanical failure is the likely cause
  • Visible cracks or warping on the двигатель block — from severe overheating or fatigue; not repairable in the field

Продолжение эксплуатации с этими симптомами грозит дальнейшими повреждениями. Своевременно замените деталь.
