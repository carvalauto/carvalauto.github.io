What is this Center Console Interior Air Conditioner AC Control Panel But?

This is an aftermarket replacement air conditioning control panel button assembly for the center console of a Toyota Land Cruiser from 2007–2015. It is the interface you use to adjust cabin temperature, fan speed, and airflow direction, and it houses the push buttons and internal contacts that send those commands to the climate control system.

Over time, the buttons on a Land Cruiser's AC control panel can wear out, stick, or stop responding altogether. Signs Your AC Control Panel Needs Replacement

These are common indicators that the button assembly or its internal contacts have failed.

  • Buttons require excessive force to press
  • Some buttons do not respond at all
  • Temperature or fan speed changes unexpectedly
  • Display flickers or dims when buttons are pressed
  • Buttons feel loose or stick in the pressed position
  • Intermittent operation over bumps or rough roads

If you experience any of these symptoms, stop using the panel to avoid further damage and have it inspected by a qualified technician.

Tools Required

1

Disconnect battery

Turn off the ignition and disconnect the negative battery cable to prevent electrical shorts.

2

Remove center console trim

Use a plastic trim tool to carefully pry off the trim pieces surrounding the center console and AC control panel.

3

Extract mounting screws

Remove the screws securing the AC control panel using a Phillips screwdriver or socket set.

4

Unplug electrical connectors

Press the release tabs and disconnect the wiring harnesses from the back of the panel.

5

Remove old panel

Pull the old control panel out of the console and set it aside.

6

Install new panel

Position the new panel into the console, reconnect the harnesses, and secure it with the mounting screws.

7

Reassemble trim

Snap the trim pieces back into place, making sure all clips engage.

8

Test operation

Reconnect the battery and test all buttons and functions before driving.
