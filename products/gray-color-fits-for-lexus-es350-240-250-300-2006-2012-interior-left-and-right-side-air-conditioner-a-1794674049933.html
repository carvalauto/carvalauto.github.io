Signs You Need New Vent Outlets

These symptoms indicate the dashboard vent outlets may be damaged or worn.

  • Vent louvers are broken or missing
  • Airflow direction cannot be adjusted
  • Vent flap will not stay open or closed
  • Visible cracks or damage to the vent housing
  • Rattling or buzzing noise from the vent area
  • Vent does not sit flush with the dashboard

If the vent housing is cracked, debris can enter the ductwork; replace promptly to maintain proper airflow.
