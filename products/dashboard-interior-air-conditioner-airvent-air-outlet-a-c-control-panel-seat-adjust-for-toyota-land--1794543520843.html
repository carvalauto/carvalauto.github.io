Signs you need a new dashboard vent panel

These symptoms indicate the center vent and control panel assembly is worn or damaged.

  • Air vent louvers are broken or stuck and won't direct airflow
  • Cracks or missing pieces in the dashboard trim around the vents
  • Seat adjustment switches are loose or fall out of the panel
  • Rattling or buzzing noise from the center dash area
  • Vent housing no longer clips securely into the dashboard
  • Visible damage or discoloration on the vent surround

If you notice broken plastic pieces or loose switches, stop using the affected controls until the panel is replaced to avoid further damage.

Installation Guide

Installing this OEM 5567060070 is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 45–60 minutes.

Estimated Time

Skill Level

Tools Required
Trim removal tool, Phillips screwdriver, 10mm socket and ratchet, panel clip pliers
1

Disconnect battery

Turn off the ignition and disconnect the negative battery terminal to avoid electrical shorts while working on the seat switch wiring.

2

Remove trim panels

Use a trim removal tool to carefully pry off the surrounding dashboard trim pieces that cover the center vent assembly. Work slowly to avoid breaking clips.

3

Remove mounting screws

Locate and remove the screws securing the vent and control panel assembly using a Phillips screwdriver or 10mm socket.

4

Disconnect wiring and ducts

Pull the assembly forward slightly, then disconnect the electrical connectors for the seat adjustment switches and any air duct connections.

5

Transfer components

If your new panel does not include switches, transfer the seat adjustment switches and any reusable clips from the old unit to the new one.

6

Install new panel

Connect the wiring and ducts to the new assembly, then seat it into the dashboard and secure it with the mounting screws.

7

Reinstall trim

Reinstall the surrounding trim panels, pressing them firmly into place until the clips engage.

8

Reconnect battery and test

Reconnect the negative battery terminal. Turn on the ignition and test the air vent airflow and seat adjustment switches for proper operation.
