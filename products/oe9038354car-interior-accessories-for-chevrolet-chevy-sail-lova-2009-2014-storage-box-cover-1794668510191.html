Signs you need a new storage box cover

A damaged console cover is usually obvious once you look for these common issues.

  • The cover is cracked or split across the top
  • The hinge is broken and the lid no longer stays attached
  • The latch does not catch, so the cover falls open while driving
  • The cover is missing entirely, leaving an open storage box
  • The cover sits loose or rattles over bumps
  • The surface is worn, faded, or peeling from sun exposure

A loose or broken cover can let small items roll out of the storage box and into the footwell while driving; replace it promptly.

Tools Required
Trim removal tool, Phillips screwdriver (if the console trim is screwed), clean cloth
1

Open the storage box

Open the existing cover fully so you can see the hinge area and any retaining pins or screws.

2

Remove the old cover

If the cover is held by pins, gently pry them out with a trim removal tool. If screws are present, remove them with a Phillips screwdriver and lift the old cover away.

3

Clean the mounting area

Wipe the hinge points and surrounding console trim with a clean cloth to remove dust and debris.

4

Test-fit the new cover

Align the new cover with the hinge points and check that it sits flush and opens and closes without binding.

5

Secure the cover

Reinstall the retaining pins or screws in the reverse order of removal. Do not overtighten screws.

6

Check operation

Open and close the cover several times to confirm the latch catches and the lid moves smoothly.
