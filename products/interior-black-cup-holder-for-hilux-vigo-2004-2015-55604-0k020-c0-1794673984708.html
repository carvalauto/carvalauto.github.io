Signs Your Cup Holder Needs Replacement

A failing cup holder is usually obvious in daily use, but these are the common clues that the unit is worn or broken.

  • Cups or bottles lean or tip over while driving
  • The cup holder sits loose in the console and rattles
  • Visible cracks in the plastic body or retaining clips
  • The unit pops out of the console over bumps
  • Spring-loaded arms or tabs no longer grip drinks
  • Missing pieces or broken mounting tabs on the original part

If the cup holder is loose, avoid placing hot drinks in it until the unit is replaced, as a spill can damage console electronics or upholstery.

Locate the retaining screws or clips holding the cup holder. Remove screws with a Phillips-head screwdriver or release clips with the trim tool.

4

Lift out the old unit

Pull the old cup holder straight up and out of the console. Wipe the mounting area with a shop rag to remove dust and debris.

5

Set the new black cup holder into the console opening and check that it sits flush and aligns with the mounting points.

Reinstall the retaining screws or press the clips into place until the cup holder is firmly seated.

7

Snap the surrounding console trim back into position, making sure all clips engage.

8
