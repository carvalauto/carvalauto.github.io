What is OEM 55422-06040?

Signs you need a new DVD panel

The DVD panel is a cosmetic and mounting component, so problems usually show up as visible damage or poor fit rather than mechanical failure.

  • Visible cracks or breaks in the plastic trim
  • Scratches or scuffs that won't polish out
  • Loose or rattling trim around the screen
  • Gaps between the panel and dashboard surface
  • Broken mounting tabs or clips
  • Faded or discolored finish compared to the rest of the interior

If the panel is loose, check for broken clips or missing screws before replacing it, as the mounting points may also need attention.

Installation Guide

Installing this OEM 55422-06040 is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 30-45 minutes.

Tools Required
Plastic trim removal tool, Phillips-head screwdriver, socket set (if bolts are used)
1

Disconnect the battery

Turn off the ignition and disconnect the negative battery cable to avoid electrical shorts while working near the radio and climate controls.

2

Remove the old panel

Use a plastic trim tool to gently pry the edges of the existing DVD panel away from the dashboard, working around the perimeter to release the clips. If screws are present, remove them with a Phillips-head screwdriver.

3

Disconnect electrical connectors

If the panel has switches or connectors attached, press the release tabs and unplug them. Set the old panel aside.

4

Transfer components

If your new panel does not include switches or vents, transfer them from the old panel by removing their mounting screws and clipping them into the new panel.

5

Test-fit the new panel

Align the new panel with the dashboard openings and press it into place gently. Do not force it; check that all clips and screw holes line up.

6

Secure the panel

Reinstall any screws and press the clips fully into place. Reconnect any electrical connectors you removed.

7

Reconnect the battery and test

Reconnect the negative battery cable, start the vehicle, and check that all controls and the screen function normally. Verify the panel is flush and secure.
