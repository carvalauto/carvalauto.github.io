Signs Your Steering Wheel Control Button Needs Replacement

If you notice any of these issues with your Land Cruiser LC300 steering wheel controls, the button assembly may need to be replaced.

  • Buttons are unresponsive or require excessive force to activate
  • Intermittent operation of audio or cruise control functions
  • Physical damage such as cracks or missing button caps
  • Sticky or worn button surfaces
  • Backlighting failure (if equipped)
  • Error messages or warning lights related to steering wheel controls

Before replacing the button assembly, ensure the issue is not caused by a blown fuse or a loose connection elsewhere in the steering column.

Installation Guide

Installing this part is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 45–60 minutes.

Tools Required
Trim removal tool, Torx screwdriver set, socket set, plastic pry bar, electrical tape
1

Disconnect Battery

Turn off the ignition and disconnect the negative terminal of the battery to prevent accidental airbag deployment.

2

Remove Airbag

Locate the two small access holes on the sides of the steering wheel. Insert a thin tool to release the airbag retaining clips, then carefully pull the airbag module away and disconnect its connector.

3

Remove Steering Wheel Trim

Use a trim removal tool to pry off the decorative trim around the button cluster. Take care not to scratch the plastic.

4

Remove Old Button Assembly

Remove the screws securing the button cluster (typically Torx or Phillips). Disconnect the electrical connector and lift the old assembly out.

5

Install New Button Assembly

Connect the electrical connector to the new button cluster, then secure it with the screws. Reinstall the trim.

6

Reinstall Airbag

Reconnect the airbag connector and carefully push the airbag module back into place until the clips engage. Reconnect the battery.

7

Test Functions

Turn on the ignition and test all buttons for proper operation. Ensure no warning lights remain on.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this an factory-style part?
No, this is an aftermarket replacement part. It does not have an factory-style number and is not affiliated with Toyota.
Will this fit my 2020 Land Cruiser LC300?
This part is designed for the 2021–2023 model years. It may not fit 2020 or earlier models due to potential differences in steering wheel design.
Do I need to disconnect the battery to install this?
Yes, always disconnect the battery before working on the steering wheel to avoid accidental airbag deployment.
Does this include the airbag?
No, this is only the button assembly. The airbag is not included and must be reused from your original steering wheel.

No programming is typically required. The buttons should work once connected, but some vehicle settings may need to be reset via the infotainment system.
