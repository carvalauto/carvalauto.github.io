Signs Your Center Dash Vent Needs Replacement

A failing center vent can affect both comfort and the look of your interior; watch for these common indicators.

  • Airflow from the center vent is weak or uneven
  • Louvers are broken, stuck, or won't stay in position
  • Vent rattles or squeaks while driving
  • Visible cracks or damage to the vent housing
  • Vent does not direct air where intended
  • Dashboard trim around the vent is loose or misaligned

If you notice any of these symptoms, replacing the vent promptly ensures proper climate control and maintains your vehicle's interior appearance.
