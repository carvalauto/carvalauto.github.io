  • Upper storage box lid area damaged or missing

Refreshing a worn Hilux Vigo interior

Signs the upper dashboard cover needs replacing

These are the common visible clues that the upper dash cover on a Hilux Vigo is damaged or no longer doing its job.

  • Visible cracks or splits in the top dashboard plastic
  • Broken or missing upper storage box lid/cover
  • Faded, chalky, or discolored dash-top surface
  • Scratches or scuffs on the upper dash panel
  • Rattles or loose fit from broken mounting tabs
  • Gaps around the upper dash area where the cover should sit flush

If the cover is cracked or loose, avoid placing objects on the dash top until it is replaced, as small items can fall into the gap.

Installing this OEM 50540OK010 is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 30-45 minutes.

Tools Required
Trim removal pry tools, Phillips screwdriver, socket set, clean microfiber cloth
1

Disconnect the battery

Switch off the ignition and disconnect the negative battery terminal as a precaution before working around the dashboard.

2

Remove surrounding trim

Use a plastic trim pry tool to gently release any clips and trim pieces around the upper dash and storage box area.

3

Remove fasteners

Locate and remove the screws or bolts holding the original upper cover in place, keeping them together for reuse.

4

Lift out the old cover

Carefully lift the old cover away, watching for clips or tabs that may still be attached to the dash.

5

Clean the mounting area

Wipe the dash-top surface and mounting points with a clean microfiber cloth to remove dust and loose debris.

6

Fit the new cover

Position the replacement cover, align the tabs and mounting holes, and press it into place without forcing it.

7

Reinstall fasteners and trim

Refit the screws or bolts and reattach the surrounding trim pieces, checking that the cover sits flush.

8

Reconnect and check

Reconnect the negative battery terminal and check that the cover is secure and does not rattle.
