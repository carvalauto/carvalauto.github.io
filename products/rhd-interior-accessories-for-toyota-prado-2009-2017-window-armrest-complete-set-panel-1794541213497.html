When to replace your Prado armrest trim

These signs suggest the original window switch armrest panels are worn, damaged, or simply due for an interior upgrade.

  • Cracked or broken plastic around the window switches
  • Scuffed or faded armrest surface on the door card
  • Loose trim that moves when you rest your arm on it
  • Missing or damaged clips causing the panel to sit unevenly
  • Visible wear from years of use on the driver-side armrest
  • Wanting a softer, upholstered surface instead of bare plastic

If the window switches themselves are failing electrically, address that fault separately; these panels are trim parts and do not repair switch or wiring problems.

Installation Guide

Installing this part is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 30-45 minutes.

⏱️
Estimated Time

Skill Level

🛠️
Tools Required
Trim removal tool, Phillips screwdriver, clean microfiber cloth
1

Prepare the door panel

Park on a level surface, switch off the ignition, and lower the window. Wipe the area around the switch trim with a microfiber cloth.

2

Remove the original trim

Use a trim removal tool to gently pry up the original armrest/switch panel, working around the edges to release the retaining clips. Disconnect the window switch connector if the panel requires it.

3

Test-fit the new panel

Set the new armrest panel in place without forcing it, checking that the clip locations and switch cutout line up with the door card.

4

Reconnect and seat the panel

Reconnect the window switch connector, then press the new panel down evenly until the clips engage. Do not hammer or force the trim.

5

Repeat on the remaining doors

Work through the rest of the set one door at a time, keeping removed screws and clips organised.

6

Check operation

Operate each window switch to confirm normal function, and press along the panel edges to ensure it sits flush.
