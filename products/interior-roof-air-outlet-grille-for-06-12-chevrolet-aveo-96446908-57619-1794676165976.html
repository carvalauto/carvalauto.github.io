Signs you need a new roof air outlet grille

A damaged roof vent grille can affect both the appearance and function of your Aveo's interior.

  • Visible cracks or breaks in the plastic grille
  • Grille is missing or loose in the headliner
  • Airflow from the roof vent is weak or uneven due to damaged louvers
  • Discoloration or fading that doesn't match the interior
  • Rattling or buzzing noise from the roof vent area
  • Grille no longer stays clipped into the headliner

If the grille is loose or broken, avoid forcing it back into place, as this can damage the headliner or duct.
