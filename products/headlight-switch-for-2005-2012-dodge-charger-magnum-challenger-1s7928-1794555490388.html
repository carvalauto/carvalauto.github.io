Signs Your Headlight Switch Needs Replacement

A failing headlight switch can cause a range of lighting problems that affect safety and convenience.

  • Headlights do not turn on or off reliably
  • Instrument panel lights flicker or stay dim
  • Dome lamp fails to illuminate when the switch is activated
  • Switch feels loose or makes a clicking noise
  • Intermittent operation of headlights while driving
  • Dashboard lights do not respond to dimmer adjustment

If you experience any of these symptoms, have the switch inspected promptly to avoid driving with faulty lighting.
