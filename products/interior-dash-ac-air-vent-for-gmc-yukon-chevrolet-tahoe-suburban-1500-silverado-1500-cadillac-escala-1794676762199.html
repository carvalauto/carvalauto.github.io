Signs You Need a New Dash Air Vent

A failing dash vent can affect cabin comfort and appearance; watch for these common issues.

  • Airflow from the vent is weak or uneven regardless of fan speed
  • Louvers are broken, missing, or cannot be adjusted
  • Vent rattles or squeaks over bumps
  • Housing is cracked or detached from the dashboard
  • Vent flaps do not stay in the desired position
  • Visible damage or discoloration that does not clean up

If you notice coolant smell or wet carpet near the vent, stop driving and have the HVAC system inspected—this may indicate a heater core leak, not just a vent problem.
