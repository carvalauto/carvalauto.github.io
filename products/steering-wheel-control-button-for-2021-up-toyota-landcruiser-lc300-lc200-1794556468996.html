Signs You Need a New Steering Wheel Control Button

These symptoms indicate the steering wheel switch may be failing and need replacement.

  • Buttons do not respond when pressed
  • Audio volume or track controls work intermittently
  • Phone call buttons fail to activate
  • Cruise control buttons stick or require excessive force
  • Visible cracks or missing button caps
  • Backlighting on buttons is dim or non-functional

If multiple buttons fail simultaneously, check the clock spring and wiring harness before replacing the switch.

Installation Guide

Installing this part is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 30-45 minutes.

Tools Required
Plastic trim tool, T25 Torx driver, socket set, flathead screwdriver
1

Disconnect Battery

Turn off the ignition and disconnect the negative battery terminal to prevent accidental airbag deployment.

2

Remove Airbag

Locate the two small access holes on the back of the steering wheel, insert a flathead screwdriver, and release the airbag retaining clips. Carefully unplug the airbag connector.

3

Remove Old Switch

Using a T25 Torx driver, remove the screws securing the old switch assembly to the steering wheel spoke. Unplug the electrical connector.

4

Install New Switch

Plug the wiring harness into the new switch, position it on the steering wheel, and secure with the original screws.

5

Reinstall Airbag

Reconnect the airbag connector and press the airbag module back into place until the clips engage.

6

Test Function

Reconnect the battery, turn on the ignition, and test all buttons for proper operation.
