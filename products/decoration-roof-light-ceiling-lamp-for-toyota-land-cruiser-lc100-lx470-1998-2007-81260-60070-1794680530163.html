Signs Your Roof Ceiling Lamp Needs Replacement

A failing ceiling lamp can affect both cabin lighting and the finished look of the headliner.

  • Interior dome light does not turn on or flickers
  • Cracked or broken lens cover
  • Lamp housing loose or hanging from the headliner
  • Burnt or discolored bulb socket
  • Missing or damaged switch button
  • Visible corrosion on the electrical contacts

If the lamp is cracked or the housing is loose, replace it promptly to prevent further headliner damage or electrical issues.
