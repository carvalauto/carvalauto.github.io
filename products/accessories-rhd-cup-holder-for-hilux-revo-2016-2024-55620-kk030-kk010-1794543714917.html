Signs Your Cup Holder Needs Replacement

A failing cup holder can be more than an inconvenience—it can lead to spills and a messy console.

  • Cup holder is cracked or broken
  • Spring-loaded arms no longer grip cups
  • Holder rattles or feels loose in the console
  • Missing or damaged retaining clips
  • Cups tip over easily while driving
  • Visible damage to the plastic housing

If your cup holder is loose or broken, replace it promptly to avoid spills that can damage console electronics.
