Signs your overhead console needs replacement

The overhead console is a simple part, but when it fails you will notice it every time you get in the car at night.

  • The dome light or reading lights do not come on when you open the door
  • One or both reading lights flicker or stay dark even with a new bulb
  • The lens is cracked, missing, or so cloudy that light output is poor
  • The housing is cracked or the mounting tabs are broken, so it hangs loose
  • The switch feels loose or does not click between positions
  • The beige plastic has faded or discolored compared to the rest of the headliner

Before replacing the whole console, check the bulb and the fuse first—sometimes a dead reading light is just a blown bulb or a bad door switch, not the assembly itself.

Installation Guide

Installing this OEM 92820-2EXXX is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 20-30 minutes.

Tools Required
Trim pry tool, Phillips-head screwdriver, small flathead screwdriver
1

Disconnect the battery

Pop the hood and disconnect the negative battery cable so you do not short the interior lighting circuit while working.

2

Remove the lens

Use a small flathead screwdriver or trim tool to gently pry the lens off the console. This exposes the bulbs and the mounting screws.

3

Remove the mounting screws

Take out the Phillips screws that hold the console to the roof. Keep them in a cup so they do not get lost in the headliner.

4

Lower the console and unplug it

Pull the console down carefully and press the release tab on the wiring connector to separate it from the body harness.

5

Transfer the bulbs

If your new console does not include bulbs, pull the bulbs from the old unit and push them into the new socket. Wear gloves so you do not get oil on the glass.

6

Plug in and test

Connect the wiring harness to the new console, then reconnect the battery and test the dome and reading lights before screwing it in.

7

Mount the new console

Tuck the wiring back into the roof opening, press the console into place, and install the mounting screws. Do not overtighten, or you can crack the plastic.

8

Reinstall the lens

Snap the lens back onto the new housing, making sure the tabs click into place.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does this fit my 2008 Kia Sportage?
The listing covers the 2004–2010 Sportage range, but the primary fitment data is for the 2004–2007 model. The safest approach is to compare the shape of your existing console and the connector with the photos before ordering. If your 2008 has the same console shape, it should fit.

Does it come with bulbs?
The listing does not specify whether bulbs are included. Plan on transferring the bulbs from your old console, or pick up the correct bulbs separately. The sockets are designed for the standard bulbs used in the Sportage overhead console.
Will the beige color match my interior?
The housing is beige, which is the common interior color for the Sportage of this era. If your headliner is gray or tan, the beige may not be an exact match. Check the color of your existing console before ordering.
Can I replace just the lens instead of the whole console?
If only the lens is cracked, you may be able to find a lens separately. But if the housing is cracked, the switch is bad, or the contacts are corroded, replacing the complete assembly is the more reliable fix.
Why do my reading lights not work after installation?
Check the connector first—make sure it is fully seated. Then check the bulb and the fuse. If the dome light works but the reading lights do not, the switch inside the console may be faulty; contact the seller for a replacement.
