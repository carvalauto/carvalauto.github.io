Signs You Need a New Dash Air Vent

A failing vent usually shows up as physical damage or poor airflow control rather than a warning light.

  • Louvers are cracked, missing, or stuck in one position
  • The vent rattles or buzzes when the blower is on
  • Airflow cannot be directed up, down, or side to side
  • The vent housing is loose in the dashboard opening
  • The open/close thumbwheel no longer works or is broken
  • Pieces of the vent trim are falling out

If the vent is loose and you see gaps around the dashboard opening, check for broken mounting tabs before installing the new unit.

Installation Guide

Installing this OEM 96887307 is a straightforward process. The full job takes about 30-45 minutes.

Tools Required
Plastic trim pry tool, Phillips-head screwdriver, flat-head screwdriver, flashlight
1

Remove the trim panel

Use a plastic trim pry tool to gently release the dashboard trim panel or bezel that surrounds the vent. Work slowly to avoid cracking the plastic clips.

2

Access the vent screws

Locate the screws holding the old vent to the dashboard. They are usually Phillips-head and may be hidden behind the trim you just removed.

3

Remove the old vent

Take out the screws, then pull the old vent straight out of the opening. If it resists, check for any remaining clips or tabs holding it in place.

4

Transfer any hardware

If the new vent does not come with mounting screws, move the original screws to the new unit. Check that the new vent has the same mounting points.

5

Install the new vent

Slide the new vent into the dashboard opening and align the screw holes. Start the screws by hand to avoid cross-threading, then tighten them snugly.

6

Reinstall the trim

Press the trim panel or bezel back into place until the clips snap. Make sure all edges sit flush.

7

Test the vent

Turn on the blower and check that air flows through the vent and that the louvers move freely in all directions. Adjust the thumbwheel to confirm open and close operation.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will this vent fit both the left and right sides of the dashboard?
This listing is for one vent assembly with reference number 96887307. The fitment data does not specify left or right, so compare the shape and mounting points of your original vent to the product photo before ordering.
Does this vent come with the louvers and adjustment wheel?
Yes, it is a complete vent assembly that includes the housing, directional louvers, and the open/close mechanism as shown. It is not just the outer trim ring.
My vent rattles but the louvers are fine. Will this fix it?
A rattle is often caused by worn pivots or broken mounting tabs. Since this is a new complete assembly, it replaces those worn parts and should stop the rattle if the noise was coming from the vent itself.
Can I install this without removing the whole dashboard?
Yes. On these models, the vent can usually be removed by taking off the surrounding trim panel or bezel and unscrewing the vent from the front. You do not need to remove the entire dashboard.
Is this an OEM part?
It is a new replacement part with reference number 96887307. It is not described as an OEM part by the supplier, so treat it as an aftermarket replacement that fits the listed vehicles.
What should I do if the new vent feels loose in the opening?
Check that all mounting screws are installed and that the trim panel is fully clipped in place. If the opening itself is damaged, the vent may not sit tight; inspect the dashboard for cracks or missing clips.
