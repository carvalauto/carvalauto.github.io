92101-1R520 Halogen Headlight for 2015-2016 Hyundai Accent

Why the 92101-1R520 Headlight Is a Smart Choice for Your Hyundai Accent

If you own a 2015 or 2016 Hyundai Accent, you already know it's a reliable commuter. But when a headlight goes out or gets cracked, you need a replacement that fits right and works with your car's wiring. The 92101-1R520 halogen headlight with LED accents is designed specifically for the Hyundai Accent from 2014 onward, including 5-door, GLS, GS, and SE sedan models. This isn't a universal fit—it's an OEM-style unit that plugs into your existing harness and matches the factory look.

In this guide, I'll cover what this headlight is, when you should replace it, how to choose the right one, and some installation tips to make the job easier. Whether you're a DIYer or just want to understand what your mechanic is talking about, this will help.

What Is the 92101-1R520 Headlight?

The 92101-1R520 is a halogen headlight assembly for the Hyundai Accent. It's a complete unit—housing, lens, reflector, and bulb socket—that replaces the original headlight on the driver or passenger side. This particular version also includes LED lights, which typically serve as accent or daytime running lights, depending on your Accent's trim. The halogen projector or reflector handles the main low and high beams, while the LED strips add a modern touch and improve visibility.

Because it's an OEM part number, you can be confident it's the same specification as what came from the factory. That means no cutting, splicing, or custom mounting. It bolts into the same locations and uses the same connectors.

When Should You Replace Your Accent's Headlight?

Headlights don't last forever. Here are the most common reasons to replace the 92101-1R520 assembly:

  • Collision damage: Even a minor fender bender can crack the lens or break the mounting tabs. A cracked lens lets moisture in, which can corrode the reflector and shorten bulb life.
  • Yellowed or cloudy lens: Over time, UV exposure makes plastic lenses hazy. You can polish them, but if they're deeply oxidized or crazed, replacement is the better fix.
  • Bulb failure that won't stay fixed: If you've replaced the bulb and it still doesn't work, the socket or wiring inside the housing may be damaged. A new assembly solves that.
  • Moisture inside the housing: Condensation is normal to a degree, but standing water or fog that won't clear means the seal is compromised. That can lead to electrical issues.
  • Upgrade to LED accents: If your Accent came with plain halogen lights, this unit adds LED strips for a more modern look and better daytime visibility.

If you notice dimming lights, flickering, or a headlight that aims poorly no matter how you adjust it, the housing itself might be the problem. In that case, the 92101-1R520 is a direct replacement.

Compatibility: Which Hyundai Accent Models Does This Fit?

This headlight is designed for the Hyundai Accent from 2014 and newer. Specifically, it fits:

  • Hyundai Accent 5-door models (2014+)
  • Hyundai Accent GLS (2014+)
  • Hyundai Accent GS (2014+)
  • Hyundai Accent SE sedan (2014+)

It's listed for 2015 and 2016 in the product title, but the 2014 model year uses the same fitment. Always double-check your exact model year and trim to be safe. If you have a 2014 Accent, this will work. If you have a 2017 or newer, it likely won't—the body style changed.

One important note: this is a halogen headlight with LED accents. It's not a full LED or HID conversion. If your Accent came with factory HID or LED headlights, this may not be compatible with your wiring without additional modules. For most Accents with halogen lights from the factory, it's a direct swap.

How to Choose the Right Headlight for Your Accent

When shopping for a replacement headlight, keep these points in mind:

1. Match the OEM number

The 92101-1R520 is the exact OEM number for this assembly. If you see a different number, it might be for a different trim or model year. Cross-reference your VIN or old headlight's part number to be sure.

2. Decide if you need driver or passenger side

Headlight assemblies are side-specific. The 92101-1R520 typically corresponds to one side (often the driver's side, but verify with the seller). Make sure you're ordering the correct side for your car.

3. Consider the LED feature

This unit includes LED lights. If your Accent didn't have LED accents from the factory, you may need to check if your wiring supports them. In most cases, the LED strip is powered by the same connector, but some vehicles require a resistor or relay to prevent hyperflash or error codes. Research your specific trim.

4. Check for included bulbs

Some assemblies come with bulbs, some don't. The 92101-1R520 may or may not include the halogen bulbs. If not, you'll need to transfer your existing bulbs or buy new ones. LED accents are built-in.

5. Quality matters

Aftermarket headlights vary in quality. Look for units that use UV-resistant polycarbonate lenses and sealed housings to prevent moisture. OEM-style assemblies like this one are generally a safe bet.

Installation Tips for the 92101-1R520

Replacing a headlight on a Hyundai Accent is a straightforward job that most DIYers can handle with basic tools. Here's a quick rundown:

  • Disconnect the battery: Always start by removing the negative terminal to avoid electrical shorts.
  • Remove the front bumper (or not): On some Accents, you can access the headlight bolts by removing the bumper cover. On others, you might be able to reach them by removing the grille or inner fender liner. Check your service manual for the exact procedure.
  • Unbolt the old headlight: Typically, there are 3-4 bolts holding the assembly. Remove them and gently pull the headlight forward.
  • Disconnect the wiring: There will be one or more connectors for the main harness and the LED strip. Press the tabs and pull them apart.
  • Transfer bulbs if needed: If the new assembly doesn't include bulbs, move them from the old unit. Be careful not to touch the glass of halogen bulbs with bare fingers—oil can cause them to fail prematurely.
  • Install the new headlight: Reverse the removal steps. Connect the wiring first, then slide the assembly into place and tighten the bolts.
  • Test before reassembling: Turn on the lights to make sure everything works—low beam, high beam, and LED accents. Also check that the turn signal works.
  • Aim the headlights: After installation, you should aim the headlights to avoid blinding other drivers. Park on a level surface facing a wall about 25 feet away and adjust the vertical and horizontal aimers according to your owner's manual.

If you're not comfortable removing bumper covers or aiming headlights, it's worth having a shop do it. But for many Accent owners, this is a 1-2 hour job.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will the 92101-1R520 fit my 2016 Hyundai Accent SE sedan?

A: Yes, it fits the 2016 Accent SE sedan, as well as 5-door, GLS, and GS models from 2014 onward. Just confirm you're ordering the correct side.

Q: Does this headlight come with bulbs?

A: That depends on the seller. Some listings include halogen bulbs; others don't. The LED accent lights are built into the assembly. Check the product details before ordering.

Q: Can I install this headlight myself?

A: If you have basic hand tools and some patience, yes. The job involves removing some trim or the bumper cover, unbolting the old headlight, and plugging in the new one. Just remember to aim the headlights afterward.
